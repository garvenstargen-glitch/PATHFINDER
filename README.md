# PATHFINDER

An Outer Wilds mod by **Garvenstargen**. Look at anything in the game and the top-left
corner tells you what it's made of.

## What it shows

- **Path** — the object's name, its body, and its full hierarchy
  (e.g. `SolarSystem / CaveTwin_Body / Terrain / Rock_07`).
- **Meshes** — every mesh on it and its children, with vertex counts.
- **Textures** — each material, its texture and size, and the shader.
- **Scripts** — the scripts attached to it and to its parents.

It also shows layer, tag, distance and body. Whatever you look at turns **green** so
you know exactly which thing the readout is about.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **F3** | Cycle the panel: everything → textures → meshes → scripts → off |
| **X** | Select what you're looking at (press again to remove it) |
| **R** | Clear every selection |
| **F4** | Open the selected list: **1–9** copy one, **C** copy all, **F4** close |

Selections stay green until you remove them, you can have up to 100 at once, and the
list is remembered after you quit. Select keys can be changed in
**Options → Mods → PATHFINDER**.

## Works great with

- **[FreeCam](https://outerwildsmods.com/mods/freecam)** — fly anywhere and keep
  reading, selecting and copying. PATHFINDER follows whatever camera you're using.
- **[CheeseTools](https://outerwildsmods.com/mods/cheesetools)** — invincibility,
  infinite fuel and oxygen, frozen supernova, so you can study anything up close
  without dying or running out of air.

Both are separate mods — install them from the Mod Manager. Neither is required.
